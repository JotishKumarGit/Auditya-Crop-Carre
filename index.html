Welcome to Auditya Crop Care

Auditya Crop Care is a premier manufacturer and exporter of high-quality agrochemicals based in Chennai, Tamil Nadu. Established in 2015, we specialize in the bulk production of insecticides, fungicides, and herbicides at competitive prices.

With 32 years of industry experience under the leadership of our Proprietor, Mr. Kaliyamoorthy.G, we have built a reputation for delivering cost-effective crop protection solutions without compromising on quality. Our modern manufacturing facility located at 26-B4, SIDCO Industrial Estate, Pattaravakkam, Ambattur, Chennai produces effective and reliable agrochemicals for domestic and international markets.

We are committed to providing farmers with affordable, high-performance crop protection products that deliver excellent results and better yields. As direct manufacturers, we offer the best quality agrochemicals at low prices for bulk purchases.
